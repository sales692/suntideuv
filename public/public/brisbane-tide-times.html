Today's Brisbane Tide Information

Brisbane sits on the east coast of Queensland and experiences semi-diurnal tides, meaning there are typically two high tides and two low tides each day.

Tide predictions are important for boating, fishing, surfing and beach activities across Moreton Bay and the Brisbane River.

The data displayed below uses the nearest official Queensland tide gauge located at Brisbane Bar.

Why Brisbane Tide Times Matter

Knowing the tide schedule helps people plan activities safely and effectively.

  • Fishermen often prefer fishing during tide changes.
  • Boaters need adequate depth to navigate the Brisbane River and Moreton Bay.
  • Surfers often look for certain tide heights depending on local breaks.
  • Beachgoers benefit from knowing when tides are rising or falling.
