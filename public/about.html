About

Simple daily ocean and sun information

Sun Tide UV Moon provides quick daily information including:

  • Sunrise and sunset times
  • Tide predictions
  • UV index forecasts
  • Moon phase

The goal of the site is to provide fast and simple information for people who spend time outdoors including beach visitors, fishermen, surfers and boaters.

The website automatically determines the closest tide station in Queensland and displays the relevant tide information for that location.

Data Sources

  • Queensland Government tide predictions
  • Open Meteo weather data
  • Sunrise Sunset API

This website is an independent project designed to make daily environmental information easy to access.
